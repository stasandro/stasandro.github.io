Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What this tool is

Orbit Neighbors uses a small private application that uploads cartoon videos we created to the Orbit Neighbors YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. The application is operated only by the channel owner.

Information we access

When the owner signs in with Google, the application receives permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to read the name of that channel. It does not access the owner's email, contacts, files, or any other Google data.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal information about viewers or any other users. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sent to any third party.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. The application talks only to Google and YouTube services, to upload videos.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Our cartoons are made for children and marked "Made for Kids". We do not collect any data from children or anyone else.
